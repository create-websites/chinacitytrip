"Discover Shanxi's Top Attractions"

Main Places to See and Visit in Shanxi

Place Description
Pingyao Ancient City A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Pingyao Ancient City is a well-preserved example of a traditional Han Chinese city. Visitors can explore the city walls, temples, and traditional courtyard houses.
Yungang Grottoes Another UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Yungang Grottoes are a series of caves and statues carved into the mountainside. The grottoes date back to the 5th century and are considered a masterpiece of Buddhist art.
Mount Wutai One of the Four Sacred Mountains of Buddhism, Mount Wutai is a pilgrimage site for Buddhists from around the world. The mountain is home to many temples and monasteries, as well as stunning natural scenery.
Hukou Waterfall The Hukou Waterfall is the second-largest waterfall in China and is located on the Yellow River. Visitors can take a boat ride to get up close to the waterfall and enjoy the stunning views.
Shanxi Museum The Shanxi Museum is home to a vast collection of artifacts and exhibits related to the history and culture of Shanxi. Visitors can learn about the region's ancient past and see many rare and valuable artifacts.
