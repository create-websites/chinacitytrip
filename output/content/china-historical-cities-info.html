The Seat of Power

Not all historical cities are created equal. While Beijing is the face of modern China, Xi'an (Chang'an) held the title of Capital for the longest duration, spanning the powerful Han and Tang dynasties. This chart compares the "Time in Power" for the Four Great Ancient Capitals.

Did you know?

Luoyang is often overlooked but served as the primary or secondary capital for 13 different dynasties, making it the "City of Peonies and Emperors."
