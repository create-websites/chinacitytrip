Discover Shaanxi's Natural Wonders

Shaanxi is a province in China that is known for its rich history and culture. While it may not be the first place that comes to mind when thinking of beaches, lakes, and rivers, there are still some beautiful destinations to explore. Here are some of the best:

  • Huashan Scenic Area: While not a traditional beach, this area is known for its stunning mountain views and hiking trails. It's best to visit in the summer months when the weather is warm and dry.
  • Huanghe River: This is the second-longest river in China and is a popular spot for river cruises. While it's not recommended for swimming, it's still a beautiful sight to see.
  • Qinling Mountains: This mountain range is home to several lakes and rivers, including the Han River and the Wei River. While they may not be suitable for swimming, they offer stunning views and are a great place to hike and explore.
  • Hukou Waterfall: This is the largest waterfall on the Yellow River and is a popular tourist destination. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can take a boat ride to get up close to the waterfall.
  • Qinling Zhongnanshan National Forest Park: This park is home to several lakes and rivers, including the Lishan River and the Taibai Lake. While swimming is not recommended, visitors can enjoy hiking and exploring the park's natural beauty.
  • Qinling Mountains National Botanical Garden: This botanical garden is home to several lakes and streams, including the Xianyang Lake and the Fengxiang River. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can enjoy the park's beautiful scenery and learn about the local flora and fauna.
