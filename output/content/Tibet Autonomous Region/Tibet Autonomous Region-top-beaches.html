Best Beaches, Lakes, and Rivers in Tibet Autonomous Region

Name Description When to Visit Swimming
Yamdrok Lake A stunning turquoise lake surrounded by snow-capped mountains and traditional Tibetan villages. May to October No swimming allowed
Namtso Lake One of the highest saltwater lakes in the world, with crystal-clear blue water and breathtaking views. May to September No swimming allowed
Lhasa River A scenic river that runs through the heart of Lhasa, with beautiful bridges and historic buildings along its banks. May to October No swimming allowed
Yangbajain Hot Springs A natural hot spring located in a picturesque valley, with pools of varying temperatures and stunning mountain views. Year-round Swimming allowed
Draksum Tso Lake A serene lake surrounded by lush forests and snow-capped mountains, with a small island in the middle. May to October No swimming allowed
