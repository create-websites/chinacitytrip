"Scenic Train Journeys in Yunnan: Affordable to Luxury"

Train Journey Special Features Best Time to Visit Affordability
Yunnan-Vietnam Railway Stunning mountain views, passes through ethnic minority villages Spring and Autumn Mid-range
Dali-Lijiang Railway Views of Erhai Lake and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Year-round Affordable
Kunming-Dali Railway Views of Cangshan Mountain and Erhai Lake Year-round Affordable
Shangri-La Express Luxury train with private cabins, passes through Tiger Leaping Gorge and Shangri-La Spring and Autumn Expensive

Yunnan is home to some of the most scenic train journeys in China. The Yunnan-Vietnam Railway is a must-see, with stunning mountain views and the opportunity to pass through ethnic minority villages. The best time to visit is in the spring or autumn, and the cost is mid-range. The Dali-Lijiang Railway is another popular option, with views of Erhai Lake and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. This train journey is affordable and can be enjoyed year-round. The Kunming-Dali Railway is also a great choice, with views of Cangshan Mountain and Erhai Lake. This train journey is also affordable and can be enjoyed year-round. For those looking for a luxury experience, the Shangri-La Express is a private train with cabins and passes through Tiger Leaping Gorge and Shangri-La. The best time to visit is in the spring or autumn, but it is more expensive than the other train journeys.
