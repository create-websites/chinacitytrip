"Discover Yunnan's Best Lakes and Rivers"

Destination When to Visit Swimming
Dianchi Lake April to October Yes
Erhai Lake April to October Yes
Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Glacier Park June to September No
Shangri-La's Napa Lake May to October Yes
Yangtze River April to October Yes

Yunnan is home to some of the most beautiful natural wonders in China. Here are some of the best beaches, lakes, and rivers to visit:

  • Dianchi Lake: Located in Kunming, Dianchi Lake is the largest freshwater lake in Yunnan. It's a popular spot for swimming, boating, and fishing.
  • Erhai Lake: This beautiful lake is located in Dali and is surrounded by stunning mountains. It's a great spot for swimming, boating, and hiking.
  • Jade Dragon Snow Mountain Glacier Park: This park is home to the southernmost glacier in the Northern Hemisphere. While you can't swim here, it's a great spot for hiking and taking in the stunning views.
  • Shangri-La's Napa Lake: This lake is located in the Tibetan town of Shangri-La and is surrounded by beautiful grasslands and mountains. It's a great spot for swimming, boating, and hiking.
  • Yangtze River: The Yangtze River is the longest river in Asia and is a popular spot for swimming, boating, and fishing.
