"Discover Yunnan's Top 5 Must-Visit Destinations"

Place Description
Lijiang Old Town A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Lijiang Old Town is a well-preserved ancient town with traditional Naxi architecture and culture. Visitors can stroll through the narrow streets, visit the Mu Palace, and enjoy the local food and music.
Stone Forest A natural wonder of limestone formations, the Stone Forest is a unique landscape that resembles a forest made of stone. Visitors can hike through the maze of towering rocks and explore the caves and waterfalls.
Tiger Leaping Gorge One of the deepest gorges in the world, Tiger Leaping Gorge offers breathtaking views of the Yangtze River and the surrounding mountains. Visitors can hike along the high trail or the low trail, and enjoy the stunning scenery.
Shangri-La A Tibetan town with a rich culture and history, Shangri-La is known for its beautiful monasteries, traditional architecture, and stunning natural scenery. Visitors can explore the Songzanlin Monastery, hike to the nearby mountains, and experience the local Tibetan culture.
Dali Old Town A charming ancient town with a mix of Bai and Han culture, Dali Old Town is famous for its traditional architecture, local food, and handicrafts. Visitors can stroll through the cobblestone streets, visit the Three Pagodas, and enjoy the views of Erhai Lake.
