"Discover Yunnan's Natural Wonders: Lakes, Hiking, and More"

Scenic Places, Lakes, and Hiking in Yunnan

Yunnan is a province in southwestern China known for its stunning natural beauty. From snow-capped mountains to crystal-clear lakes, Yunnan has something for everyone. Here are some of the best places to visit:

Place Best Months to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
Lijiang April to June, September to November Stay in a traditional Naxi-style guesthouse Visit the Old Town, hike in the nearby mountains, and explore the surrounding villages Be prepared for crowds during peak season
Dianchi Lake March to May, September to November Stay in a lakeside resort Take a boat ride on the lake, visit the nearby temples, and hike in the surrounding hills Be aware of water pollution in the lake
Tiger Leaping Gorge March to May, September to November Stay in a guesthouse in the nearby town of Qiaotou Hike the gorge trail, visit the nearby villages, and enjoy the stunning views Be prepared for steep and challenging hiking trails
Shangri-La April to June, September to November Stay in a Tibetan-style guesthouse Visit the Songzanlin Monastery, hike in the nearby mountains, and explore the surrounding Tibetan villages Be prepared for high altitude and cold temperatures
