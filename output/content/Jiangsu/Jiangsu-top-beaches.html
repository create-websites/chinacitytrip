"Discover the Best Swimming Destinations in Jiangsu Province"

Destination When to Visit Swimming
Yangcheng Lake April to October Yes
Slender West Lake April to October No
Laoshan Island May to September Yes
Qinhuai River April to October No

Jiangsu province in China is home to some of the most beautiful beaches, lakes, and rivers in the country. Here are some of the best destinations to visit:

  • Yangcheng Lake: This lake is famous for its delicious hairy crabs and is a popular spot for fishing and boating. Visitors can also swim in the lake during the warmer months.
  • Slender West Lake: This scenic lake is surrounded by gardens and historic buildings. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can take a boat ride to enjoy the views.
  • Laoshan Island: This island is known for its clear waters and sandy beaches. It's a great spot for swimming and sunbathing during the summer months.
  • Qinhuai River: This river runs through the city of Nanjing and is a popular spot for boat rides and sightseeing. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can enjoy the beautiful scenery and historic landmarks along the riverbanks.
