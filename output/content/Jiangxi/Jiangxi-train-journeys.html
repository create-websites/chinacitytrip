"Affordable Train Journeys Through China's Scenic Countryside"

Train Journey What's Special When to Visit Affordability
Jinggangshan Scenic Railway Offers stunning views of the Jinggang Mountains and passes through several tunnels and bridges. Best visited in spring or autumn when the weather is mild and the scenery is at its best. Affordable, with tickets starting at around 50 RMB.
Ganzhou to Longyan Railway Travels through the beautiful Wuyi Mountains and passes by several historic sites, including the ancient city of Chongwu. Best visited in spring or autumn when the weather is mild and the scenery is at its best. Affordable, with tickets starting at around 60 RMB.
Nanchang to Fuzhou Railway Travels through the scenic countryside of Jiangxi and Fujian provinces, passing by several historic towns and villages. Best visited in spring or autumn when the weather is mild and the scenery is at its best. Affordable, with tickets starting at around 70 RMB.
Jiujiang to Jingdezhen Railway Travels through the beautiful Lushan Mountains and passes by several historic sites, including the ancient city of Jingdezhen. Best visited in spring or autumn when the weather is mild and the scenery is at its best. Affordable, with tickets starting at around 50 RMB.
