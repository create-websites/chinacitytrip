"Discover China's Natural and Cultural Wonders"

Place Description
Mount Lu One of China's most famous mountains, known for its natural beauty and cultural significance. Visitors can hike, take a cable car, or enjoy the view from a boat on the nearby lake.
Poyang Lake The largest freshwater lake in China, home to a variety of bird species and a popular spot for boating and fishing.
Ganzhou Hakka Tulou A UNESCO World Heritage Site, these unique circular buildings were constructed by the Hakka people as a form of defensive architecture.
Wuyuan A picturesque town known for its ancient architecture, beautiful countryside, and vibrant rapeseed flower fields in the spring.
Jingdezhen The "Porcelain Capital" of China, famous for its high-quality ceramics and porcelain production. Visitors can tour the factories and learn about the history of the craft.
Longhu Mountain A sacred Taoist mountain with numerous temples and scenic spots, including the famous "Fairy Cave" and "Dragon Palace".
