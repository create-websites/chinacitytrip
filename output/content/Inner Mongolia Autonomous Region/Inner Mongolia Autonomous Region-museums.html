Discover Inner Mongolia's Rich History and Culture

Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region

Name Description Location
Inner Mongolia Museum The Inner Mongolia Museum showcases the history and culture of the region, including exhibits on the Mongolian people, their customs, and their way of life. Visitors can see artifacts from the Neolithic period to the present day, including traditional clothing, weapons, and musical instruments. Hohhot
Wudangzhao Monastery The Wudangzhao Monastery is a Tibetan Buddhist monastery that dates back to the 17th century. It is known for its beautiful architecture, including the Hall of the Five Hundred Arhats, which features 500 life-sized statues of Buddhist disciples. Visitors can also see the monastery's collection of Buddhist scriptures and other artifacts. Baotou
Genghis Khan Mausoleum The Genghis Khan Mausoleum is a memorial to the famous Mongolian leader, who is buried here. Visitors can see the tomb, as well as exhibits on Genghis Khan's life and legacy. The mausoleum is located in a scenic area with beautiful views of the surrounding countryside. Ordos
Xilamuren Grassland The Xilamuren Grassland is a vast expanse of grassland that is home to many Mongolian herders. Visitors can experience traditional Mongolian culture, including horseback riding, archery, and watching performances of Mongolian music and dance. The grassland is also a great place to see the stars at night. Xilinhot
Zhaojun Tomb The Zhaojun Tomb is a tomb of a Han dynasty princess who married a Xiongnu leader. Visitors can see the tomb, as well as exhibits on the history of the Han-Xiongnu relationship. The tomb is located in a scenic area with beautiful views of the nearby mountains. Hohhot
