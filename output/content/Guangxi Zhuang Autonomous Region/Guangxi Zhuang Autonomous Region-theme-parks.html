"Experience Thrills and Scenery at Guangxi's Theme Parks"

Theme Parks and Amusement Parks to Visit in Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region

Park Name Location Months to Visit Popular Rides
Window of the World Nanning Year-round Miniature replicas of famous landmarks from around the world
Happy Valley Nanning Year-round Extreme roller coasters and water rides
Chimelong Ocean Kingdom Beihai Year-round Marine animal shows and interactive exhibits
Chimelong Paradise Guilin Year-round Thrilling roller coasters and water rides
Yangshuo Park Yangshuo March to November Boat rides on the Li River and scenic views of karst mountains

Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region offers a variety of theme parks and amusement parks for visitors to enjoy. Window of the World in Nanning features miniature replicas of famous landmarks from around the world, while Happy Valley offers extreme roller coasters and water rides. Chimelong Ocean Kingdom in Beihai is a must-visit for marine animal shows and interactive exhibits, and Chimelong Paradise in Guilin is known for its thrilling roller coasters and water rides. Yangshuo Park in Yangshuo is a great option for those looking for a more scenic experience, with boat rides on the Li River and views of karst mountains. Most parks are open year-round, but Yangshuo Park is only open from March to November.
