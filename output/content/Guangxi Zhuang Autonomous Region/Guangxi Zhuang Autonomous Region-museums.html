Discover Guangxi's Rich History and Natural Wonders

Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region

Name Description Location
Guangxi Museum The Guangxi Museum showcases the history, culture, and natural resources of Guangxi. It has over 50,000 exhibits, including fossils, ethnic costumes, and ancient artifacts. Nanning
Liuzhou Museum The Liuzhou Museum has a collection of over 30,000 cultural relics, including pottery, bronze ware, and calligraphy. It also has a section dedicated to the history of the Liuzhou area. Liuzhou
Detian Waterfall The Detian Waterfall is the largest waterfall in Asia and is located on the border between China and Vietnam. It is a popular tourist destination and is surrounded by lush forests and karst mountains. Chongzuo
Longji Rice Terraces The Longji Rice Terraces are a series of terraced rice paddies that have been cultivated for over 700 years. They are located in the mountains and offer stunning views of the surrounding landscape. Longsheng
Reed Flute Cave The Reed Flute Cave is a natural limestone cave that is illuminated by colorful lights. It has a variety of stalactites and stalagmites, and is named after the reeds that grow outside the cave. Guilin
