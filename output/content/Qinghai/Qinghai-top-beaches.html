"Discover Qinghai's Best Water Wonders"

Best Beaches, Lakes, and Rivers in Qinghai

Name Description When to Visit Swimming
Koko Nor Lake Also known as Qinghai Lake, it is the largest saltwater lake in China and a popular tourist destination. The lake is surrounded by snow-capped mountains and grasslands, making it a picturesque location. May to October Yes, but only in designated areas
Chaka Salt Lake A stunning salt lake with crystal clear blue water and a white salt crust. The lake is surrounded by mountains and is a popular spot for photography. May to October No
Tongtian River Also known as the "River of Heaven", it is a scenic river that flows through deep gorges and past towering mountains. The river is popular for rafting and kayaking. May to September Yes, but only in designated areas
Qilian Mountains A range of mountains that offer stunning views of glaciers, lakes, and grasslands. The mountains are also home to many ethnic minority groups and their unique cultures. May to October No
