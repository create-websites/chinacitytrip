"Discover Hainan's Best Theme Parks and Amusement Parks"

Theme Parks and Amusement Parks to Visit in Hainan

Park Name Location Months to Visit Popular Rides
Haichang Fantasy Town Sanya Year-round Dragon's Treasure Roller Coaster, Atlantis Submarine Adventure
Wuzhizhou Island Resort Sanya Year-round Zip Line, Bungee Jumping, Water Sports
Hainan Tropical Wildlife Park and Botanical Garden Haikou Year-round Safari Park, Bird Park, Butterfly Park
Hainan Happy World Haikou Year-round Roller Coasters, Ferris Wheel, Water Park
Qionghai Boao Oriental Paradise Qionghai Year-round Water Park, Ferris Wheel, Bumper Cars
Evergreen Park Sanya November to April Roller Coaster, Ferris Wheel, Water Park

Hainan is a great destination for theme park and amusement park enthusiasts. With year-round warm weather, visitors can enjoy these parks any time of the year. Haichang Fantasy Town in Sanya is a popular choice with its Dragon's Treasure Roller Coaster and Atlantis Submarine Adventure. Wuzhizhou Island Resort, also in Sanya, offers a variety of outdoor activities such as zip lining, bungee jumping, and water sports. The Hainan Tropical Wildlife Park and Botanical Garden in Haikou is a great option for animal lovers with its safari park, bird park, and butterfly park. Hainan Happy World in Haikou has a range of rides including roller coasters, a ferris wheel, and a water park. Qionghai Boao Oriental Paradise in Qionghai is another option with a water park, ferris wheel, and bumper cars. Evergreen Park in Sanya is only open from November to April but offers a roller coaster, ferris wheel, and water park.
