Discover Hainan's Rich History and Nature

Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Hainan

Name Description Location
Hainan Museum The Hainan Museum showcases the history, culture, and natural resources of Hainan Island. It has over 20 exhibition halls with artifacts and displays that date back to the Neolithic Age. Haikou City
Wugong Temple The Wugong Temple is a historic temple that was built in honor of the five officials who were exiled to Hainan during the Tang Dynasty. It has beautiful architecture and is surrounded by lush greenery. Haikou City
Yanoda Rainforest Cultural Tourism Zone The Yanoda Rainforest Cultural Tourism Zone is a nature reserve that has hiking trails, waterfalls, and a cultural village that showcases the customs and traditions of the Li and Miao ethnic groups. Sanya City
Qiongtai Academy The Qiongtai Academy is a historic academy that was built during the Song Dynasty. It was a center for education and culture in Hainan and has beautiful architecture and gardens. Wenchang City
Dongjiao Coconut Plantation The Dongjiao Coconut Plantation is a scenic area that has over 5000 coconut trees. Visitors can take a tour of the plantation, taste fresh coconut water, and learn about the history and uses of coconuts in Hainan. Wanning City
