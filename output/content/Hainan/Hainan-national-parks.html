Wildlife Parks, State Parks, and National Parks to Visit in Hainan

Park Name Months to Visit Time Required Important Tips
Yalong Bay Tropical Paradise Forest Park Year-round Half-day to full-day Wear comfortable shoes and bring insect repellent.
Dongzhaigang National Nature Reserve November to April Half-day to full-day Bring warm clothing and binoculars for bird watching.
Wuzhishan National Forest Park Year-round Full-day to multiple days Prepare for hiking and camping. Bring appropriate gear and supplies.
Qixianling Hot Springs National Forest Park Year-round Half-day to full-day Bring swimwear and towels for the hot springs. Wear comfortable shoes for hiking.
Hainan Tropical Wildlife Park and Botanical Garden Year-round Half-day to full-day Bring sunscreen and a hat for sun protection. Wear comfortable shoes for walking.
