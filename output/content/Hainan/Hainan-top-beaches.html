"Discover Hainan's Best Beaches, Lakes, and Rivers"

Best Beaches, Lakes, and Rivers in Hainan

Name Description When to Visit Swimming
Yalong Bay One of the most popular beaches in Hainan, known for its crystal-clear waters and white sand. It's surrounded by luxury hotels and resorts. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from October to May when the weather is cooler. Yes, it's safe for swimming.
Sanya Bay A long stretch of beach with calm waters and a wide range of water sports activities. It's also a popular spot for watching the sunrise and sunset. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from November to April when the weather is cooler. Yes, it's safe for swimming.
Dadonghai Beach A family-friendly beach with shallow waters and a variety of water sports activities. It's also surrounded by restaurants and shops. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from November to April when the weather is cooler. Yes, it's safe for swimming.
Wuzhizhou Island A small island off the coast of Hainan with crystal-clear waters and a variety of water sports activities. It's also known for its coral reefs and underwater scenery. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from November to April when the weather is cooler. Yes, it's safe for swimming.
Daming Lake A scenic lake in the heart of Jinan city, surrounded by temples, pavilions, and gardens. It's also a popular spot for boating and fishing. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from April to October when the weather is warmer. No, swimming is not allowed.
Nanwan Monkey Island An island off the coast of Hainan known for its wild macaque monkeys. It's also surrounded by beaches and coral reefs. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from November to April when the weather is cooler. Yes, it's safe for swimming.
Yangtze River The longest river in China, known for its scenic beauty and cultural significance. It's also a popular spot for river cruises and water sports activities. Year-round, but the best time to visit is from April to October when the weather is warmer. No, swimming is not allowed.
