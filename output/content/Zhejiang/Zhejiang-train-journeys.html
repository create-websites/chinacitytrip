Zhejiang province in China offers some of the most scenic train journeys in the country. Here are some of the best train journeys in Zhejiang:

  • Hangzhou-Shaoxing Railway: This train journey takes you through the beautiful Qiantang River and ancient towns. The best time to visit is during spring and autumn when the weather is pleasant. This train journey is affordable and perfect for budget travelers.
  • Hangzhou-Ningbo High-Speed Railway: This train journey offers stunning views of Hangzhou Bay and Xiangshan Mountain. You can take this train journey year-round, and it is mid-range in terms of affordability.
  • Hangzhou-Huangshan High-Speed Railway: This train journey takes you through tea plantations and Huangshan Mountain. The best time to visit is during spring and autumn. This train journey is mid-range in terms of affordability.
  • Hangzhou-Jinhua Railway: This train journey offers views of bamboo forests and ancient villages. You can take this train journey year-round, and it is affordable.
  • Hangzhou-Changsha High-Speed Railway: This train journey takes you through Qiandao Lake and Wuyi Mountain. The best time to visit is during summer and autumn. This train journey is expensive, but worth it for the stunning views.
