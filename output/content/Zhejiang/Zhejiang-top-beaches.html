"Discover Zhejiang's Best Water Getaways"

Best Beaches, Lakes, and Rivers in Zhejiang

Name Description When to Visit Swimming
Qiandao Lake A man-made lake with crystal clear water and over 1,000 islands. It's a popular spot for water sports and fishing. Summer months (June-August) for swimming and water activities. Spring and fall for hiking and sightseeing. Yes, the lake is safe for swimming.
Xiangshan Beach A long stretch of sandy beach with clear water and beautiful views of the mountains. It's a popular spot for swimming and sunbathing. Summer months (June-August) for swimming and water activities. Spring and fall for hiking and sightseeing. Yes, the beach is safe for swimming.
West Lake A large freshwater lake in Hangzhou with beautiful scenery and historic sites. It's a popular spot for boating and sightseeing. Year-round, but spring and fall are the best times to visit for mild weather and beautiful foliage. No, swimming is not allowed in the lake.
Fuchun River A scenic river that runs through Hangzhou and surrounding areas. It's a popular spot for rafting and sightseeing. Summer months (June-August) for rafting and water activities. Spring and fall for hiking and sightseeing. Yes, the river is safe for rafting and swimming in certain areas.
