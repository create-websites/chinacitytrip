Discover the Best of Hangzhou: Lakes, Canals, Mountains, and Towns

Place Description
West Lake A UNESCO World Heritage Site, West Lake is a beautiful freshwater lake surrounded by gardens, temples, and pagodas. Visitors can take a boat ride on the lake, stroll through the gardens, and enjoy the scenic views.
Hangzhou Grand Canal The longest canal in the world, the Hangzhou Grand Canal is a marvel of engineering and a testament to China's ancient civilization. Visitors can take a boat ride along the canal and see the historic buildings and bridges that line its banks.
Mount Putuo A sacred Buddhist mountain, Mount Putuo is home to several temples and shrines. Visitors can hike to the top of the mountain and enjoy the panoramic views of the surrounding area.
Wuzhen Water Town A picturesque water town, Wuzhen is known for its ancient architecture, canals, and bridges. Visitors can take a boat ride through the town and see the traditional houses and shops that line the waterways.
Nanxun Water Town Another beautiful water town, Nanxun is known for its well-preserved architecture and gardens. Visitors can stroll through the town and see the historic buildings and bridges that date back to the Ming and Qing dynasties.
