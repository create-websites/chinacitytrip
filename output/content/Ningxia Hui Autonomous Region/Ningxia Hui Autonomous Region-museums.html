Discover Ningxia's Rich History: Museums & Historic Sites

Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region

Name Description Location
Ningxia Museum The museum showcases the history and culture of Ningxia, including exhibits on the Silk Road, the Hui ethnic group, and ancient artifacts. Yinchuan
Western Xia Tombs The tombs are the final resting place of the Western Xia rulers and their families. The site includes over 9,000 tombs and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Yinchuan
Shuidonggou Site The site is an important archaeological discovery, with evidence of human habitation dating back over 30,000 years. Visitors can see artifacts and learn about the ancient cultures that lived in the area. Shizuishan
Haibao Pagoda The pagoda is a symbol of Ningxia and is located on a hill overlooking the Yellow River. Visitors can climb to the top for panoramic views of the surrounding area. Shizuishan
Helan Mountains Rock Art The rock art in the Helan Mountains is over 6,000 years old and depicts scenes of hunting, dancing, and daily life. The site is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Yinchuan
