Best Cities in Guizhou

City Months to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
Guiyang March to May, September to November Grand Mercure Hotel, Pullman Guiyang Hotel Visit Jiaxiu Tower, Qingyan Ancient Town, and Huaxi Park Try the local cuisine, especially the spicy hotpot
Anshun April to October Howard Johnson Resort, Anshun Explore Huangguoshu Waterfall, Dragon Palace Cave, and Tianlong Ancient Town Bring comfortable shoes for hiking and walking
Kaili May to October Grand Metropark Hotel, Kaili Visit Miao and Dong ethnic minority villages, attend local festivals, and try traditional food Respect local customs and traditions, and dress appropriately
Zunyi March to May, September to November Wyndham Grand Plaza Royale, Zunyi Visit the Red Army Memorial Museum, Zunyi Conference Site, and Baihua Lake Learn about the city's revolutionary history and culture
