"Discover Guizhou's Scenic Hiking Trails and Lakes"

Scenic Places, Lakes, and Hiking in Guizhou

Months to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
March to May and September to November Guiyang, Anshun, or Kaili Visit Huangguoshu Waterfall, hike in Fanjing Mountain, explore the Miao villages, and enjoy the scenery of Qiandongnan Bring comfortable hiking shoes, warm clothing, and insect repellent. Be prepared for some steep and challenging hikes.
June to August Guiyang or Anshun Visit the stunning Huanguoshu Waterfall, explore the Dragon Palace Cave, and enjoy the cool temperatures of the underground river. Bring light clothing and sunscreen. Be prepared for crowds during peak tourist season.
December to February Guiyang or Kaili Visit the Xijiang Miao Village, explore the ancient town of Qingyan, and enjoy the winter scenery of the Huangguoshu Waterfall. Bring warm clothing and be prepared for cold temperatures. Some attractions may be closed during the winter months.
