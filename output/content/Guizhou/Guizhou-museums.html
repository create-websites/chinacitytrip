Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Guizhou

Name Description Location
Guiyang Hongfu Temple Ancient temple with a history of over 400 years, featuring traditional Chinese architecture and beautiful gardens. Guiyang
Miao Village of Xijiang Ancient village of the Miao ethnic minority, featuring traditional wooden houses, unique customs, and colorful festivals. Leishan County
Qingyan Ancient Town Well-preserved ancient town with a history of over 600 years, featuring traditional Chinese architecture, temples, and gardens. Guiyang
Guizhou Provincial Museum Museum showcasing the history, culture, and natural resources of Guizhou, featuring artifacts, exhibits, and interactive displays. Guiyang
Zhenyuan Ancient Town Well-preserved ancient town with a history of over 2,000 years, featuring traditional Chinese architecture, temples, and scenic views. Zhenyuan County
Chishui Danxia Landform Natural wonder featuring colorful rock formations, waterfalls, and forests, formed over millions of years by geological processes. Chishui City
