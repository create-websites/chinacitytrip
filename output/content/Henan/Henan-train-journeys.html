"Discover China's Best Train Journeys"

Train Journey Special Features When to Visit Affordability
Shaolin Temple Scenic Area Tourist Train Passes through the beautiful Songshan Mountain range and stops at the famous Shaolin Temple, known for its martial arts and Buddhist culture. Best visited in the spring or autumn for mild weather and fewer crowds. Affordable, with tickets starting at around 50 RMB ($7 USD).
Luoyang to Xi'an High-Speed Train Travels through the ancient capital of Luoyang, known for its Longmen Grottoes, and ends in Xi'an, home to the Terracotta Warriors. Can be visited year-round, but spring and autumn offer the most pleasant weather. Relatively affordable, with tickets starting at around 200 RMB ($28 USD).
Yuntai Mountain Scenic Area Tourist Train Offers stunning views of the Yuntai Mountain range, including waterfalls, canyons, and forests. Best visited in the autumn for the colorful foliage. Affordable, with tickets starting at around 60 RMB ($8 USD).
Zhengzhou to Luoyang High-Speed Train Travels through the bustling city of Zhengzhou and ends in Luoyang, known for its ancient history and cultural sites. Can be visited year-round, but spring and autumn offer the most pleasant weather. Relatively affordable, with tickets starting at around 100 RMB ($14 USD).
