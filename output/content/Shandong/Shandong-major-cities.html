Best Cities in Shandong

City Months to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
Qingdao May to October Hotels near the beach or city center Visit the beaches, climb Mount Lao, try local seafood, visit the Tsingtao Brewery Be prepared for crowds during peak season, bring sunscreen and comfortable shoes for hiking
Jinan April to October Hotels near the city center or Daming Lake Visit Daming Lake, Baotu Spring, Thousand Buddha Mountain, Shandong Museum Be prepared for hot and humid weather in the summer, bring comfortable shoes for walking
Qufu April to November Hotels near Confucius Temple or Kong Family Mansion Visit Confucius Temple, Kong Family Mansion, Cemetery of Confucius Be respectful of the cultural significance of the sites, dress modestly and remove shoes when entering temples
Weifang April to October Hotels near Kite Square or city center Visit Kite Museum, Yangjiabu Folk Culture Village, Fuhua Amusement Park Be prepared for hot and humid weather in the summer, bring sunscreen and insect repellent
