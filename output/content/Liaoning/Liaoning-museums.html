Discover Liaoning's Rich History and Culture

Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Liaoning

Name Description Location
Shenyang Imperial Palace The palace was built in 1625 and served as the residence of the Qing emperors. It is one of the few remaining imperial palaces in China and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Shenyang
Dalian Modern Museum The museum showcases modern art and design from around the world. It has a collection of over 3,000 works of art and is housed in a modern building designed by Japanese architect Tadao Ando. Dalian
Benxi Water Caves The caves are a natural wonder with underground rivers, waterfalls, and stalactites. Visitors can take a boat ride through the caves and see the beautiful rock formations. Benxi
Anshan Jade Buddha Palace The palace is home to a 9-meter tall jade Buddha statue, which is the largest in the world. It is also a Buddhist temple and has beautiful gardens and architecture. Anshan
Liaoning Provincial Museum The museum has a collection of over 200,000 artifacts from Liaoning's history, including pottery, jade, and bronze objects. It also has exhibits on the region's natural history and culture. Shenyang
