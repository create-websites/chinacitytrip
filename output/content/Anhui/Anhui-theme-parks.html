"Thrilling Theme Parks in Anhui: Wanda, Fantawild, and More!"

Theme Parks and Amusement Parks to Visit in Anhui

Park Name Location Months to Visit Popular Rides
Wanda Theme Park Hefei March to November Warrior's Fury, Flying Over the Rainforest, Dinoconda
Happy Magic Water Cube Hefei June to September Wave Pool, Tornado, Super Bowl
Chizhou Fantawild Adventure Chizhou March to November Mayan Adventure, Jungle Racing, Flying Saucer
Wuhu Fantawild Adventure Wuhu March to November Galaxy Spin, Super Wings, Dino Rampage
Yunxi Zhongshan Park Tongling March to November Roller Coaster, Pirate Ship, Ferris Wheel

Anhui has a variety of theme parks and amusement parks to choose from. The Wanda Theme Park in Hefei is a popular choice with thrilling rides such as Warrior's Fury, Flying Over the Rainforest, and Dinoconda. The Happy Magic Water Cube, also in Hefei, is a great option for those looking to cool off during the hot summer months with attractions like the Wave Pool, Tornado, and Super Bowl. Chizhou Fantawild Adventure and Wuhu Fantawild Adventure offer a mix of exciting rides and immersive experiences, with popular attractions like Mayan Adventure, Jungle Racing, and Galaxy Spin. Yunxi Zhongshan Park in Tongling is a smaller park but still offers fun rides like the Roller Coaster, Pirate Ship, and Ferris Wheel. Most parks are open from March to November, with the exception of the Happy Magic Water Cube which is only open from June to September.
