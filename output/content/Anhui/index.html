Discover the Beauty of Huangshan and Surrounding Villages

Place Description
Huangshan Mountain Huangshan Mountain is a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its stunning natural scenery, including granite peaks, pine trees, and hot springs. Visitors can hike the mountain trails, take a cable car to the summit, and stay in one of the mountain lodges.
Hongcun Village Hongcun Village is a traditional Chinese village that has been well-preserved and is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The village is known for its unique architecture, including the Hui-style houses and the Moon Pond. Visitors can also see the ancient trees and visit the local museums.
Jiuhua Mountain Jiuhua Mountain is a sacred Buddhist mountain that is home to many temples and monasteries. Visitors can hike the mountain trails, see the ancient architecture, and learn about the Buddhist culture. The mountain is also known for its beautiful scenery, including waterfalls and forests.
Tunxi Old Street Tunxi Old Street is a historic street in the city of Huangshan that is known for its traditional architecture and local shops. Visitors can see the old buildings, buy souvenirs, and try the local cuisine. The street is also home to several museums and galleries.
Xidi Village Xidi Village is another traditional Chinese village that has been well-preserved and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The village is known for its unique architecture, including the Hui-style houses and the ancient wells. Visitors can also see the local museums and learn about the history of the village.
