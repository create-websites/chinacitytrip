"Thrilling Adventure Awaits: Top Guangdong Theme Parks"

Theme Parks and Amusement Parks to Visit in Guangdong

Park Name Location Months to Visit Popular Rides
Chimelong Paradise Panyu, Guangzhou March to November Ten Inversion Roller Coaster, Dive Coaster, Water Park
Window of the World Nanshan, Shenzhen All Year Round Miniature Replicas of World Landmarks, 5D Cinema, Roller Coaster
Happy Valley Chimelong, Guangzhou March to November Wooden Coaster, Suspended Coaster, Water Park
OCT East Yantian, Shenzhen All Year Round Mountain Coaster, Bungee Jumping, Rafting
Shenzhen Safari Park Nanshan, Shenzhen All Year Round Wildlife Safari, Animal Shows, Amusement Rides

Guangdong is home to some of the best theme parks and amusement parks in China. Chimelong Paradise in Panyu, Guangzhou is a must-visit for thrill-seekers with its Ten Inversion Roller Coaster and Dive Coaster. The park also has a water park for those who want to cool off during the hot months from March to November.

Window of the World in Nanshan, Shenzhen is a unique park that features miniature replicas of world landmarks. Visitors can also enjoy a 5D cinema and a roller coaster.

Happy Valley in Chimelong, Guangzhou is another popular park with wooden and suspended coasters, as well as a water park. The park is open from March to November.

OCT East in Yantian, Shenzhen offers a different kind of experience with its mountain coaster, bungee jumping, and rafting. The park is open all year round.

For those who love animals, Shenzhen Safari Park in Nanshan, Shenzhen is a great choice. Visitors can go on a wildlife safari, watch animal shows, and enjoy amusement rides.
