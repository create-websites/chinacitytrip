"Explore the Scenic Wonders of Guangdong: Lakes, Hiking, and More!"

Scenic Places, Lakes, and Hiking in Guangdong

Month to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
March to May Guangzhou or Shenzhen Hike in Danxia Mountain, visit the Seven Star Crags, and explore the Guangzhou Botanical Garden Bring comfortable hiking shoes and a light jacket for cooler temperatures in the mountains
June to August Stay in a lakeside resort in Zhaoqing or Qingyuan Swim in the crystal-clear waters of Baoding Shan and Dinghu Lake, hike in the scenic forests of Qingyuan, and visit the historic Kaiping Diaolou Bring sunscreen, insect repellent, and a hat to protect from the sun
September to November Stay in a traditional village in Conghua or Meizhou Hike in the Conghua Hot Spring Scenic Area, visit the Hakka Tulou in Meizhou, and explore the ancient town of Chikan Bring a light jacket for cooler temperatures in the mountains and comfortable walking shoes for exploring the villages
December to February Stay in a ski resort in Qingyuan or Meizhou Ski or snowboard in the mountains of Qingyuan or Meizhou, visit the Guangdong Folk Art Museum, and explore the historic city of Foshan Bring warm clothing and snow gear for skiing and snowboarding
