Sichuan is home to some of the most beautiful natural attractions in China. Here are some of the best beaches, lakes, and rivers to visit:

  • Jiuzhaigou Valley: This UNESCO World Heritage Site is known for its crystal-clear lakes, waterfalls, and colorful forests. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can enjoy hiking and sightseeing from April to November.
  • Emeishan Scenic Area: This area is famous for its stunning mountain scenery and Buddhist temples. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can enjoy hiking and sightseeing from May to October.
  • Daocheng Yading: This area is known for its snow-capped peaks, crystal-clear lakes, and Tibetan culture. While swimming is not allowed, visitors can enjoy hiking and sightseeing from May to October.
  • Erhai Lake: This beautiful lake is located in Dali and is a popular spot for swimming, boating, and fishing. The best time to visit is from April to October.
  • Jinsha River: This river is known for its crystal-clear waters and stunning scenery. Visitors can enjoy swimming, rafting, and fishing from June to September.
  • Mugecuo Scenic Area: This area is known for its crystal-clear lakes, waterfalls, and colorful forests. Visitors can enjoy swimming, hiking, and sightseeing from May to October.
