Discover Sichuan's Rich History: Museums and Landmarks

Museums and Historic Places to Visit in Sichuan

Name Description Location
Jinsha Site Museum The museum showcases the ancient Shu civilization and the archaeological discoveries made at the Jinsha site. Visitors can see artifacts such as gold masks, jade objects, and bronze wares. Chengdu
Sanxingdui Museum The museum displays the artifacts found at the Sanxingdui archaeological site, which dates back to the Bronze Age. Visitors can see bronze masks, ivory carvings, and gold objects that are unique to the region. Guanghan
Wuhou Shrine The shrine is dedicated to Zhuge Liang, a famous military strategist and statesman of the Three Kingdoms period. Visitors can see his statue, as well as other historical artifacts and cultural relics. Chengdu
Mount Qingcheng The mountain is a Taoist site and is known for its natural beauty and cultural significance. Visitors can see temples, pavilions, and other structures that are built into the mountain landscape. Chengdu
Leshan Giant Buddha The giant Buddha statue is carved into a cliff face and is the largest stone Buddha statue in the world. Visitors can climb up to the statue and see it up close, as well as enjoy the scenic views of the surrounding area. Leshan
