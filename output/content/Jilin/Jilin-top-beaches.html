"Discover Jilin's Best Swimming Spots"

Destination When to Visit Swimming
Chagan Lake Summer Yes
Changbai Mountain Hot Springs Year-round Yes
Longtan Mountain Scenic Area Summer Yes
Yalu River Summer Yes
Qianguoerluosi Lake Summer Yes

Jilin is home to some of the most beautiful natural attractions in China. Here are some of the best beaches, lakes, and rivers to visit:

  • Chagan Lake: This is the largest alpine lake in China and is known for its crystal-clear waters. It's a great place to swim during the summer months.
  • Changbai Mountain Hot Springs: These hot springs are open year-round and are a great place to relax and unwind. The water is said to have healing properties.
  • Longtan Mountain Scenic Area: This area is known for its beautiful waterfalls and clear mountain streams. It's a great place to cool off during the hot summer months.
  • Yalu River: This river forms the border between China and North Korea and is a popular spot for swimming and water sports during the summer months.
  • Qianguoerluosi Lake: This lake is known for its stunning scenery and is a great place to swim during the summer months.
