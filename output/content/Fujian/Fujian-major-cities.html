Discover Fujian's Best Cities

Best Cities in Fujian

City Months to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
Xiamen March to May, September to November Siming District or Gulangyu Island Visit Gulangyu Island, climb Sunlight Rock, stroll along Zhongshan Road, explore Nanputuo Temple Book accommodations in advance, bring comfortable shoes for walking
Fuzhou March to May, September to November Wuyi Square or Gulou District Visit Drum Mountain, explore Sanfang Qixiang, see the West Lake Park, visit the Fujian Provincial Museum Bring an umbrella for unexpected rain, try local snacks like fish balls and fried rice noodles
Quanzhou March to May, September to November Licheng District or Fengze District Visit Kaiyuan Temple, explore Qingjing Mosque, see the Maritime Museum, visit the Anping Bridge Bring comfortable shoes for walking, try local seafood dishes
Putian March to May, September to November Chengxiang District or Hanjiang District Visit the Meizhou Island Scenic Area, see the Mazu Temple, explore the Hanjiang Ancient City, visit the Guanghua Temple Bring sunscreen and a hat for sunny days, try local delicacies like oyster omelette and peanut soup
