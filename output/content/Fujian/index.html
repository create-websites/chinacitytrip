"Discovering Fujian: A Cultural Journey"

Place Description
Gulangyu Island A car-free island known for its colonial architecture, beaches, and museums. It is also home to the famous Piano Museum and Shuzhuang Garden.
Mount Wuyi A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its stunning natural scenery, including waterfalls, gorges, and tea plantations. Visitors can also take a bamboo raft ride down the Nine Bend River.
Tulou A unique type of circular earthen building found in the Fujian countryside. These structures were built by the Hakka people and are known for their architectural and cultural significance.
Xiamen University A beautiful campus with a mix of traditional Chinese and Western architecture. It is also home to the famous Furong Tunnel, a historic underground passage.
Qingjing Mosque A historic mosque in Quanzhou, one of the oldest and most important ports in China. It is a testament to the city's rich Islamic heritage and is still in use today.
