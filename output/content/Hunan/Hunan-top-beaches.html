"Discover Hunan's Best Water Wonders"

Best Beaches, Lakes, and Rivers in Hunan

Name Description When to Visit Swimming
Dongting Lake The second-largest freshwater lake in China, Dongting Lake is known for its scenic beauty and abundant wildlife. Visitors can take boat tours to explore the lake and its surrounding wetlands. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit, as the weather is mild and the scenery is at its most beautiful. Yes, but be aware of water quality and safety.
Yuelu Mountain Scenic Area Located in the heart of Changsha, Yuelu Mountain offers stunning views of the city and the Xiang River. Visitors can hike to the top of the mountain or take a cable car for a more leisurely ascent. Any time of year, but spring and autumn are the most pleasant. No, swimming is not allowed.
Zhangjiajie National Forest Park Famous for its towering sandstone pillars and breathtaking views, Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is a must-visit destination for nature lovers. Visitors can hike through the park's many trails or take a cable car for a bird's-eye view. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit, as the weather is mild and the scenery is at its most beautiful. No, swimming is not allowed.
Furong Ancient Town Nestled in the mountains of western Hunan, Furong Ancient Town is a charming village that has preserved its traditional architecture and way of life. Visitors can stroll through the narrow streets and admire the old buildings and temples. Any time of year, but spring and autumn are the most pleasant. No, swimming is not allowed.
Liuyang River Flowing through the city of Changsha, the Liuyang River is a popular spot for boating and sightseeing. Visitors can take a boat tour to see the city's landmarks and enjoy the scenery. Any time of year, but spring and autumn are the most pleasant. Yes, but be aware of water quality and safety.
