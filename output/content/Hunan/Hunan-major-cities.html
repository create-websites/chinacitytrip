"Discover Hunan's Top Cities: Changsha, Zhangjiajie, and Fenghuang"

Best Cities in Hunan

City Months to Visit Where to Stay What to Do Important Tips
Changsha March to May, September to November Wanda Vista Changsha, InterContinental Changsha, Grand Mercure Changsha Downtown Visit Orange Island, Yuelu Academy, Hunan Provincial Museum, Tianxin Pavilion Try local snacks like stinky tofu and spicy crayfish, be prepared for crowds during peak season
Zhangjiajie April to June, September to November Pullman Zhangjiajie, Crowne Plaza Zhangjiajie Wulingyuan, Zhangjiajie Huatian Hotel Explore Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, Tianmen Mountain, Glass Bridge, Baofeng Lake Bring comfortable shoes for hiking, be prepared for long lines at popular attractions
Fenghuang April to June, September to November Phoenix Grand Hotel, Fenghuang Xiangxi Hotel, Fenghuang Ancient Town Hotel Stroll through the ancient town, visit Hongqiao Art Museum, climb up to the South Great Wall Be respectful of local customs and traditions, try local delicacies like rice noodles and smoked bacon
